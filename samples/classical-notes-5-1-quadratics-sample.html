I. Definitions — Grammar
Copy each definition precisely beneath it. Exact language comes before reasoning.
Quadratic functionA function that can be written f(x) = ax² + bx + c, where a ≠ 0.
Vertex formf(x) = a(x − h)² + k; reveals the vertex directly.
VertexThe point (h, k); the highest or lowest point of the parabola.
Axis of symmetryThe vertical line x = h; the parabola mirrors across it.
II. Demonstration — Logic
The steps are given. Supply the reason each step is valid.

Describe the transformations of g(x) = −2(x − 3)² + 1 compared to the parent y = x². State the vertex and axis of symmetry.

1
In vertex form a(x − h)² + k:  a = −2,  h = 3,  k = 1
Why valid?
2
Vertex: (3, 1)  ·  Axis of symmetry: x = 3
Why valid?
3
|−2| = 2 > 1, so the parabola is vertically stretched by a factor of 2 — narrower than the parent.
Why valid?
4
a = −2 < 0, so the parabola is reflected over the x-axis — it opens downward.
Why valid?
5
h = 3 shifts the graph right 3;  k = 1 shifts it up 1.
Why valid?
Dialectic — Questions for Discussion

Why does (x − 3) shift the graph right when 3 is being subtracted?

How does the single value a control both the width and the direction of the parabola?

What would change — and what would stay the same — if a were −½ instead of −2?
