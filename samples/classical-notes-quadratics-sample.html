I. Definitions — Grammar
Copy each definition precisely beneath it. In classical study, exact language comes before reasoning.
Quadratic equationAn equation that can be written in the form ax² + bx + c = 0, where a ≠ 0.
DiscriminantThe expression b² − 4ac; it predicts the number and type of solutions.
Root / ZeroA value of x that makes the equation true; where the parabola meets the x-axis.
VertexThe highest or lowest point of the parabola; the turning point.
II. Demonstration — Logic
The steps are given. Supply the reason each step is valid — this is where the mathematics lives.

Solve 2x² + 8x + 6 = 0

1
a = 2,  b = 8,  c = 6
Why valid?
2
Discriminant: b² − 4ac = 64 − 48 = 16
Why valid?
3
16 > 0, so there are two real solutions.
Why valid?
4
x = (−8 ± √16) / 4 = (−8 ± 4) / 4
Why valid?
5
x = −1  or  x = −3
Why valid?
